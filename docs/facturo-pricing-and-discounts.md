# Facturo pricing and discounts

Reference for pre-sales and support. Prices are in EUR and exclude VAT. Quote only the figures on this page. Policy last updated on 1 September 2026.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Invoices included per month |
|---|---|---|
| Solo | €19 per month | 50 |
| Team | €79 per month | 500 |
| Business | €249 per month | 5,000 |
| Enterprise | Custom | From 25,000 |

Every plan includes invoice creation and sending, custom PDF templates, Stripe payments, the API, and the ERP and CRM integrations. Plans differ by invoice volume and support level. Enterprise adds a dedicated contact and contractual SLAs.

## Overage

Solo, Team and Business are billed €0.15 per invoice above the monthly allowance. Overage is on by default and can be switched off in Settings > Billing. With overage off, the API returns `ERR_QUOTA_402` once the allowance is used up, and the invoice is not created.

Enterprise contracts set their own volume terms.

## Upgrades and downgrades

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated to the day: the customer pays the difference between the two plans for the remaining days of the current month. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date.

## Discounts

Policy updated on 1 September 2026. Two automatic discounts exist, plus one that pre-sales may grant.

### Loyalty discount

3% off the monthly plan price for customers with 12 or more months of continuous subscription. Applied automatically from the 13th month. No request is needed and nothing has to be activated. A gap in the subscription resets the count.

### Welcome discount

5% off the monthly plan price for the first 3 months. New customers only. Applied automatically at sign-up. It cannot be granted to an existing account.

### Not cumulative

The loyalty discount and the welcome discount are not cumulative. A customer never holds both at the same time.

### Annual prepayment

Pre-sales may grant up to 10% off for a full year paid upfront. Anything above 10% needs a manager's approval before it is quoted, and that includes combinations of discounts that add up to more than 10%. Do not promise a combination in writing before the manager has answered.

## Old policy, do not quote

Before 1 September 2026 the loyalty discount was 5% and there was no welcome discount. Support staff must not quote the old figures, even to long-time customers who remember them. When a customer mentions 5% for loyalty, explain that the current loyalty discount is 3% and that the 5% figure is the welcome discount, which applies to new customers only.

## Handling pricing questions

Questions about Facturo's own pricing are P3 in the support playbook. Answer from this page, name the plan and the figure, and point the customer to Settings > Billing to see their current plan and usage. If the customer asks for a figure that is not on this page, say so and escalate rather than estimate.
